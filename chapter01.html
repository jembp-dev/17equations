1.1How do we find out about new information?

A fair question that we can ask ourselves is how do we find about new knowledge, how do we expand our understanding of the world.

One approach could be about understanding how the information we already have, can be used to find out about new information, or better said, to solve a real world problem, like knowing the distance between two points in a farm.
